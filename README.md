# ClauseCheck

**First-pass contract review in minutes, checked against your playbook, with a citation for every answer.**

> Demo build with synthetic data. Not legal advice.

Upload an NDA, MSA or SaaS agreement and get every clause extracted, scored against your firm's playbook, missing protections flagged, redlines drafted, and questions answered with section and page citations. Then export a review memo to Word or PDF.
